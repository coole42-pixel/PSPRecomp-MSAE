# MotorStorm: Arctic Edge native port: issues and enhancements

Sources: your latest session log (`out/motorstorm/bin/Release/MotorStormNative.log`:
4x / FXAA / 60 fps / 5,086-texture pack), measurements taken while this session's
work was being built, and the current code. Each item: what happens, evidence,
suggested fix, and impact (**H**igh / **M**edium / **L**ow).

Items marked **✅ Done** were fixed in the second or third round. Their "Suggested fix"
cell says what was implemented instead. New findings are in section 5 (N1–N12).

**Third round (latest):** P9, N1, N3, N4, N5 and N8 fixed; your pack converted to BC7 DDS
(P3). Two race runs with the BC7 pack: 60 fps throughout, no audio gaps during
the race, replacements working (`rejected=0`), and frames without a pack still
identical to the reference hashes.

## 1. Stability

| # | Issue | Evidence | Suggested fix | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Descriptor heap can run out with a texture pack.** Replacements are freed only by the memory budget, never by count. Original textures and replacements share 16,384 descriptors. When the heap is full, `get_texture` throws and the game exits. | `kDescriptors = 16384` (`motorstorm_gpu.cpp`); the throw in `get_texture`; replacements are evicted only above `budget_mb`. A full pack holds 5,086 replacements plus up to 4,096 cached originals. Small replacements stay far under 1 GB. | Count descriptors as well as bytes when evicting replacements; return the white texture instead of throwing; or reserve a separate descriptor range for replacements. | **H** |
| S2 | **The upload arena can be exhausted and end the game.** A single GE list that uploads more than about 44 MB of original textures, vertices and surface reloads throws "upload arena exhausted". | `kUploadBytes = 64 MiB`; the only guard is the 44 MB check in `gpu_submit`. GPU decoding now puts raw texture bytes in the same arena (smaller than decoded RGBA, so the pressure went down, not up). | Grow the arena on demand, or flush and wait inside `allocate` instead of throwing. | M |
| S3 | **A GPU stall over 30 s ends the game.** A driver reset (TDR), a laptop GPU switch or a sleep/resume becomes a fatal exception. | `wait_value` throws "GPU fence timeout"; `check(GetDeviceRemovedReason)` throws too. | Handle device removal: log it, recreate the device and surfaces, or show a clear error message box instead of a silent exit. | M |
| S4 | **Present errors are fatal.** Errors from the presenter thread rethrow on the emulation thread at the next present. Monitor changes or display-mode switches can return `DXGI_ERROR_DEVICE_RESET`. | `check(s.present_result, "present")` in `gpu_present`. The presenter now logs the real error (`presenter: …`) and tolerates `DXGI_ERROR_WAS_STILL_DRAWING`, but other errors are still fatal. | Treat reset statuses as recoverable: recreate the swap chain on the presenter thread. | M |
| S5 | **Crashes lose queued texture dumps and the log tail.** Shutdown runs only on a normal exit; the crash filter does not flush anything. | `main.cpp`: `textures::shutdown()` is only on the normal/exception paths. | Flush the log and stop the texture workers in the crash filter (best effort). | L |
| S6 | **A power loss mid-upscale corrupted 18 pack files.** Upscalers that write in place can destroy originals. The game then rejects those files, and the extractor can recreate them. | `SP03` had 18 unreadable PNGs (already restored). | Documentation: tell users to upscale into a separate folder; `pack-textures.ps1` already supports it. Optionally the game could log rejected files at startup. | L |

## 2. Performance and micro-stuttering

| # | Issue | Evidence | Suggested fix | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | ✅ **Done. The race ran at 30 fps instead of 60.** The frame-rate governor fell back right at race start and only restored 60 fps back at the menu. The trigger was the race-load burst: track loading, about 480 replacement uploads and texture decodes. | Before: `frame rate: now 29.97 fps` at the race's `StreamThread` start. After: no rate change during the whole race window, `guest_per_wall=0.9999`. | **Implemented:** governor samples taken during loading work are ignored, plus a 3 s quiet period after them. Loading work means replacement uploads, pack decodes, or bursts of more than 96 texture uploads/s. After a fallback, the target returns at the next scene start (the game's own timestep setter) or loading screen once the back-off has passed; mid-play only with clear headroom. | **H** |
| P2 | **Large stall when new replacements arrive.** *Partly done.* Up to 96 MB of texture uploads could happen in one frame. Each upload also creates a texture and a staging buffer on the emulation thread. | 480 uploads at race start. | **Implemented:** a cap of 8 textures / 16 MB per GE list, so a scene load spreads its uploads over a few frames. **Still open:** a persistent staging ring and placed resources instead of one committed resource and staging buffer per texture; uploads from a copy queue. | M |
| P3 | ✅ **Done (pack converted). 4× PNG pack: visible pop-in and a heavy memory load.** PNGs decoded on 2 worker threads (about 3.9 ms each) and the game built mip chains itself; the pack needed about 5.3 GB of video memory as RGBA against a 1 GB budget. | Before: 5,086 PNGs, 4.0 GB, `decode_ms=679` per race window. After: 5,086 BC7 DDS with full mips, 1,336 MB, `decode_ms≈500`, `rejected=0`. | **Implemented:** `pack-textures.ps1` now batches texconv (GPU BC7, 4.9 min for the whole pack), keeps subfolders, pads sizes that are not a multiple of 4 instead of falling back to PNG, gives alpha-zero images opaque alpha (the game then keeps its own alpha), and keeps colours unchanged (`--ignore-srgb`, see N9). Your pack is in `textures_bc7` and the INI points there; the PNG folder is untouched. More loader threads were tried and measured worse (N10). **Still open:** preloading a scene's replacements during its loading screen; automatic `budget_mb`. | M |
| P4 | ✅ **Done. Movie frames are treated as new textures every frame.** Menu videos (8888, 512×512) produced a new texture each frame, each decoded, uploaded and hashed for the pack. | Before: `texture_uploads=23261` per session. After: 3,322 uploads in the race window; `streamed_texture_updates=7565`. | **Implemented:** a texture state whose content changes 3+ times within a few frames becomes a streaming texture. It keeps one cache entry and one GPU texture, is re-decoded in place on the GPU, and skips pack hashing. Slots the game reuses for other textures now and then (track streaming) stay normal, so they still get replacements. | M |
| P5 | ✅ **Done. Texture cache throws everything away at once.** When the GE texture cache passed 4,096 entries or 256 MB it was cleared completely. | `motorstorm_ge.cpp`, list start. | **Implemented:** least-recently-used eviction down to 3,072 entries / 192 MB (a per-list clock on each entry); the most recently used textures stay. | M |
| P6 | ✅ **Done. CPU texture decoding is slow.** Every new texture was decoded with one `read_texel` call per texel. | `gpu_texture`: nested loop over `read_texel`. | **Implemented:** textures are decoded on the GPU. The raw PSP bytes and the palette are uploaded and a `DecodeCS` compute shader writes the RGBA mip, with swizzling, all 8 formats, CLUT shift/mask/start and multi-CLUT mips. It is bit-exact with the CPU path (unit test, plus identical in-game frame hashes). When a pack is active, the base level is still decoded on the CPU for hashing, but by a new fast decoder working on the copied raw bytes with a precomputed palette. `PSPRECOMP_MOTORSTORM_CPU_TEXTURE_DECODE=1` restores the old path for diagnosis. | M |
| P7 | **Pack matching holds the pack lock during hashing.** `match_replacement` hashes every row while holding the mutex that the loader threads also need, so decodes queue behind it on texture-heavy frames. | `motorstorm_textures.cpp`: `match_replacement` takes `std::lock_guard` before the row loop. | The index never changes after startup: read it without locking, or hash first and lock only for the lookups. | L |
| P8 | ✅ **Done. Dropped presentations.** 155 of 10,631 frames were not shown. | Before: `skipped_presents=155`. After: `skipped_presents=0`. | **Implemented:** "newest frame wins" presentation. The emulation thread only copies the frame into one of 3 snapshot slots and publishes it; it never waits. The presenter thread owns the swap chain: it waits for room on the display, presents the newest snapshot, and handles resizing. New `[graphics] vsync = true` (default; `false` uses tearing when supported). The log reports frames superseded by a newer one separately. | M |
| P9 | ✅ **Done. Shaders compile at every startup.** HLSL was compiled with `D3DCompile` at each launch. | `motorstorm_gpu.cpp`, `D3DCompile`. | **Implemented:** the shaders live in `host/motorstorm_gpu.hlsl` and are compiled at build time by the Windows SDK's `fxc` (pinned compiler, `/O3`, reflection and debug data stripped); the bytecode is embedded. No shader compiler runs at startup and `d3dcompiler` is no longer linked. fxc's false-positive warnings are silenced in the source, so the build is warning-free. | L |
| P10 | **Logging flushes on every line.** `log_line` takes a mutex and flushes the log file each time. Normal play writes few lines, but `[HLE]`/`[DISPATCH]` lines (800+ per session) and any trace option cause synchronous disk writes on the emulation thread. | `motorstorm_bootstrap.cpp`. | Buffer the log with a background writer and flush on error or exit. Quiet the per-thread `[HLE]` lifecycle messages unless `verbose = true`. | L |
| P11 | ✅ **Done. Replaced textures are sampled twice.** The shader sampled the original texture (for the game's alpha) and the replacement. | Shader `shade()`. | **Implemented:** when a replaced texture is fully opaque in the game, a flag (`replace.w` bit 1) skips sampling the original. The original is still sampled when the game's alpha matters. | L |
| P12 | ✅ **Done. Audio uses the oldest Windows audio API.** waveOut ran with 24 × 512-frame buffers plus an 8,192-frame queue (about 0.47 s). | `motorstorm_audio.cpp`. | **Implemented:** WASAPI shared mode, event-driven, `AUTOCONVERTPCM`, 30 ms device buffer, on a "Pro Audio" MMCSS thread. It follows default-device changes (e.g. headphones unplugged). Total latency is about 0.22 s, roughly 0.25 s less. It falls back to waveOut by itself; `[audio] api = waveout` forces it. See N1. | M |

## 3. Graphics and accuracy

| # | Item | Evidence | Suggested fix | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | **Vehicle liveries are not covered by offline extraction.** Their stored palettes are placeholder colour ramps; the game draws them with palettes from other texture objects or computed at runtime. About half of the textures drawn in a race are covered by offline extraction. | Extraction analysis: 193 of 376 palettized race textures match; the misses are mainly vehicle palette swaps. | Reverse-engineer the vehicle material → palette binding in the `.stf` model data, so the extractor writes each livery variant. Meanwhile use `dump-runtime-textures.ps1`. | M |
| G2 | **Replacement matching ignores palette alpha.** Two variants that differ only in alpha share one replacement. | Identity design (colour only). | Optional `<hash>_a<alphahash>` override files for packs that need per-alpha art. | L |
| G3 | ✅ **Done. Original textures have no enhanced filtering.** Non-replaced textures kept the PSP's exact bilinear and nearest filtering, so distant ground shimmered at 4×. | Shader `sampleLevel` (PSP-exact path). | **Implemented:** `[graphics] texture_filtering = enhanced` (default `psp`). Bilinear-filtered original textures are sampled with 8× anisotropic filtering. Textures without game mipmaps get a full chain generated on the GPU (`MipCS`, alpha-weighted). Nearest-filtered textures (HUD, pixel art) and framebuffer textures stay PSP-exact. Compared side by side in a race frame: smoother distant ground, HUD unchanged. | M |
| G4 | ✅ **Done. Only FXAA or SSAA.** SSAA4x is very costly at 4× (3840×2176 rasterization). | INI `antialiasing`. | **Implemented:** `antialiasing = SSAA2x`. It rasterizes at 1.5× per axis (about 2.25 samples per pixel; 2880×1632 at 4×) and area-weights the overlapping samples into each output pixel. Readback to the game stays exact. See N4. | L |
| G5 | ✅ **Done. Fullscreen is borderless only.** There was no refresh choice. | `motorstorm_window.cpp`. | **Implemented:** `[window] fullscreen_mode = exclusive` (default `borderless`) with `fullscreen_refresh` (Hz, 0 = desktop). The presenter thread enters exclusive mode when the fullscreen window has focus and leaves on Alt+Tab or F11. If the output refuses, it falls back to borderless. Tested by a race run at 60 Hz; Alt+Tab and F11 while exclusive still need a hands-on check. VSync: see P8. | L |
| G6 | **Movies stay at their native resolution.** Menu and intro videos are 480×272 and upscaled with plain filtering. | Movie path. | An optional sharpening or upscaling pass on movie frames, or support for replacement video files keyed by PMF name. | L |
| G7 | **Large textures from the pack are accepted without warning.** 27 pack images are 2048 px or larger (e.g. a 1920×1088 loading screen). | Pack scan. | Log replacements larger than 4× the original area; `pack-textures.ps1` could offer a size cap. | L |

## 4. Frame pacing and gameplay timing

| # | Item | Evidence | Suggested fix | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | ✅ **Done. Switching rate mid-race changes the game's timestep.** It rescaled the timestep values in place, and systems that cached the old step could jolt for a frame. | `switch_frame_rate` in `motorstorm_hle.cpp`. | **Implemented:** the target is restored only where a change cannot jolt gameplay: inside the game's own timestep setter at scene start, or during loading screens. Mid-play restores need clear headroom. A fallback can still happen mid-race, but only after a sustained deficit (T2), because the alternative is slow motion. | M |
| T2 | ✅ **Done. The governor reacts in 1 s windows.** Two slow seconds triggered a fallback. | `FrameRateGovernor`. | **Implemented:** 0.5 s samples. A fallback needs 4 consecutive slow samples (2 s sustained below 95 % real time); a single hitch of up to 1.5 s is tolerated. Samples during loading work and the 3 s after them do not count. A restore needs 6 headroom samples after the back-off. Unit-tested. | M |
| T3 | **Movie pacing assumes 29.97 fps.** Unlocked movies are timed by guest time at a fixed 3003-tick frame step. | `motorstorm_media.cpp`. | Read the frame rate from the PSMF header. | L |

## 5. New findings (second and third rounds)

| # | Item | Evidence | Suggested fix | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| N1 | ✅ **Done. Short audio gaps at the race-load transition with a texture pack.** | Logging each gap showed the game had submitted audio only 5–17 ms earlier, with 2–5 ms still in the device buffer. The WASAPI worker injected 10 ms of faded silence whenever the device fell below 5 ms, but the next block was only milliseconds away. Once that was fixed, a new `device_dry` counter showed the 0.22 s reserve really ran out (about 200 times per race start), because the game runs slightly below real time for a few seconds there. | **Implemented:** faded silence only for a real stall (no audio for 40 ms+); queue raised to 12,288 frames (0.31 s in all, still 0.16 s less than waveOut). Two race runs with the BC7 pack: no gaps during the race, `device_dry=1` (the benchmark's abrupt stop), 60 fps throughout. `PSPRECOMP_MOTORSTORM_AUDIO_QUEUE` overrides the depth. | M |
| N2 | **Replacement uploads still create one committed resource and staging buffer each.** About 0.1–1 ms per texture on the emulation thread, now at most 8 per frame. Staging in the shared upload arena was tried; the A/B was inconclusive, and the arena's flush mark can force GPU waits, so dedicated staging stays. | `get_replacement`. | Placed resources in a texture heap plus a dedicated persistent staging ring; uploads on a copy queue. | L |
| N3 | ✅ **Done. The runtime HLSL compiler miscompiled byte extraction from the constant buffer.** | `DecodeCS` / `clutEntry`. | **Implemented:** shaders are now built with a pinned compiler at build time (P9); palette reads use whole 32-bit words; the GPU/CPU decode parity test (128 cases) guards it. | L |
| N4 | ✅ **Done. SSAA2x costs the same as SSAA4x at resolution 1.** The raster scale had to be an integer. | `raster_scale`. | **Implemented:** raster scales are kept in half units, so SSAA2x is exactly 1.5× per axis at every resolution: 720×408 at 1×, 2880×1632 at 4×. Every raster pixel centre falls in exactly one native pixel (pixel r belongs to (2r+1)/half), so readback to the game stays exact. The GPU tests pass pixel-exact at all resolution × AA combinations, including the fractional 1× and 3×. | L |
| N5 | ✅ **Done. Enhanced filtering could bleed between regions of texture atlases at a distance.** | `MipCS`, `sampleEnhanced`. | **Implemented:** each draw passes the texel range its texture coordinates span. The shader caps the mip level so one mip texel stays within a quarter of that region; axes that tile the whole texture are not limited. In a race frame, atlas-mapped buildings stay as crisp as PSP filtering, and tiled ground keeps its smooth mips. | L |
| N6 | **After a mid-race fallback, 60 fps returns only at the next scene or loading screen** (or with very clear headroom). This is deliberate (T1). A long free-roam session on a slow PC may therefore stay at 30 fps until the next menu. | `restore_target` in `motorstorm_hle.cpp`. | Optionally restore at a pause-menu transition as well. | L |
| N7 | **Exclusive fullscreen cannot tear.** With `vsync = false`, borderless mode uses tearing presents for the lowest latency; exclusive mode presents with sync interval 0 but cannot use the tearing flag. | DXGI rules for `DXGI_PRESENT_ALLOW_TEARING`. | Documented trade-off: use borderless + `vsync = false` for the lowest latency. | L |
| N8 | ✅ **Done. Texture-pack hashing decoded the base level on the CPU.** | `gpu_texture`. | **Implemented:** the GPU decodes the texture (DecodeCS), copies the base level to a persistently mapped readback ring, and a worker thread hashes it once the fence completes; results are cached by texture key. The hash itself stays sequential (it defines the pack file names), so it runs on the CPU, but off the emulation thread. A full ring falls back to the fast CPU decoder. Replacements appear a frame or two later: `replaced_draws` 2,755,6xx vs 2,764,659 before (99.7 %). | L |
| N9 | ✅ **Done (found in the third round). The first BC7 conversion made the game too dark.** texconv treats PNGs as sRGB and converted them to linear for the non-sRGB `BC7_UNORM` output. | Average colour of a converted texture: 30 vs 77 in its PNG. | **Implemented:** `pack-textures.ps1` passes `--ignore-srgb`, so stored values stay unchanged; spot checks of 6 random textures match the PNG averages to within 1. The pack was re-converted. | **H** |
| N10 | **More PNG loader threads slow the game down.** Six loader threads raised race-start audio underruns from 4 to 106 and the game fell below real time: decoding competes with emulation on a laptop CPU. | A/B race runs. | Kept at 2, now at below-normal priority (`PSPRECOMP_MOTORSTORM_TEXTURE_THREADS` overrides). With the BC7 pack, loaders barely decode anything anyway. | L |
| N11 | **Benchmarks on this laptop vary a lot from run to run** (underruns 4–33 on identical builds before the N1 fix, worse right after a heavy GPU job). | Repeated `bench-race.ps1` runs. | Compare with at least two runs each and let the machine cool between them; prefer counters such as `device_dry` over single numbers. | L |
| N12 | **texconv's mip generation is not alpha-weighted.** The game's own mips (PNG path) weight colour by alpha. texconv's box filter can darken the edges of cut-out textures (fences, foliage) at a distance. | `pack-textures.ps1` uses `-m 0`. | If dark fringes show up: generate alpha-weighted mips before compression, or store such textures as PNG. | L |

## 6. Recommended order (remaining work)

2. **S3, S4:** recover from device removal and swap-chain resets instead of exiting.
3. **N2 / P3 (rest):** copy-queue replacement uploads; preload a scene's replacements during its loading screen; automatic `budget_mb`.
4. **P7, P10:** smaller CPU costs (lock-free pack index, buffered logging).
